About Me

Hey, I'm Equeshilm a 3D artist working across two worlds: modeling and animation. To me, a piece isn't finished if it only looks good standing still. I love the process of turning an empty shape into a character with personality, then watching it move and "speak" through animation.

I've focused on enviroment design, hard surface modeling character, and animation that feels smooth and expressive. On every project, I try to bring in the small details that make the result stand out not just "done," but intentional.
